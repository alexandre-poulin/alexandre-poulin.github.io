About

Alexandre Poulin is a Research Scientist at Meta Reality Labs Research, where he works on soft transducers and haptic technology. His research interests include soft transducers, smart materials, energy storage, and additive manufacturing.

He received the Ph.D. from École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL) in 2016, where his work in the Soft Transducers Laboratory focused on dielectric elastomer actuators, including miniaturized actuators for the mechanical stimulation of cell cultures. He was subsequently a postdoctoral researcher at EPFL (2016–2018) and at Empa, the Swiss Federal Laboratories for Materials Science and Technology (2019–2021), before joining Meta in April 2021. He holds B.Sc. (2009) and M.Sc. (2012) degrees in engineering physics from Polytechnique Montréal.
